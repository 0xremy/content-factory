Launched in Q1 2026 with mainnet codenamed "The Pacific Ocean," it positions itself at the convergence of three explosive trends: institutional capital inflows into crypto ($24B+ RWA market grew 266% in 2025), regulatory maturation enabling compliance-first infrastructure, and technological breakthroughs in blockchain scalability (30,000 TPS with 1-second finality).
